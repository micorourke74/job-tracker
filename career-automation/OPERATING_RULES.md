# Operating Rules

## Qualification philosophy

The goal is not maximum application count. The goal is maximum legitimate interview probability.

Do not reject a candidate because every preferred qualification is not met. Distinguish:
- mandatory requirements,
- strongly preferred requirements,
- optional wishlist requirements.

## Scoring

Score each job 0-100.

Suggested weights:
- 30: mandatory qualification fit
- 20: direct skill overlap
- 15: experience-level fit
- 10: location/work-arrangement fit
- 10: compensation/quality of opportunity
- 10: recency and likelihood posting is active
- 5: strategic value (Disney/internal mobility, recognizable SaaS, strong growth path)

Automatic actions:
- 80-100: priority application
- 65-79: apply when no material disqualifier
- 50-64: human review / secondary queue
- below 50: archive unless strategically unusual

## Application behavior

For approved-fit jobs:
- create a job-specific resume derivative from the canonical resume;
- preserve factual dates and credentials exactly;
- mirror posting terminology only when truthful;
- keep ATS formatting simple;
- answer role-specific free text concisely;
- favor employer site application over one-click repost when available.

## Mandatory stop points

Require user interaction for:
- CAPTCHA
- MFA / account authentication
- electronic signature
- legal certification
- unclear work authorization
- unclear criminal-history disclosure
- unclear clearance declaration
- disability or veteran self-identification without a standing preference
- demographic/EEO answers without a standing preference
- any personal fact not present in the approved source of truth

## Follow-up

For strong applications:
- identify recruiter/hiring-team contact when publicly available;
- prepare concise outreach;
- follow up once after a reasonable interval unless the posting says not to;
- stop outreach after rejection or explicit no-contact instruction.

## Learning loop

Track by:
- role family
- resume variant
- source
- company
- application date
- recruiter response
- screen
- interview
- assessment
- offer
- rejection
- ghosted

If 25-30 qualified applications in one lane produce no recruiter screens, change the lane, resume positioning, or targeting rules before sending another large batch.

# Recruiter / Hiring-Team Outreach Contract

Purpose: move a strong application out of the anonymous queue without spamming.

Only generate outreach when:
- the application is a strong fit, and
- a legitimate relevant contact or referral route is identified.

Rules:
- one short message
- identify the exact role
- 1–2 strongest evidence-backed fit points
- no fake familiarity
- no guilt, pressure, repeated chasing, or mass templating
- do not claim an application was submitted until submission is confirmed
- do not contact unrelated employees indiscriminately

Record contact source and outreach date in the ledger.

# Cover Letter Contract

Generate only when the employer requests one or when it materially improves the application.

Use:
- verified candidate evidence
- the tailored resume
- verified job evidence

Requirements:
- concise
- specific to the role
- no invented affinity, product usage, customer stories, or company facts
- no repetition of the entire resume
- no unsupported superlatives
- no generic AI opening paragraphs
- connect 2–3 strongest verified experiences to 2–3 important job needs
- mention company-specific facts only when sourced
- never hide a mandatory qualification gap with misleading wording

Before rendering, return the evidence IDs supporting each paragraph.
